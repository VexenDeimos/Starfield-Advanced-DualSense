## Production Presentation

### Continuous chassis

`LandVehicleControllerFeel` implements approved Option A:

- idle effectively silent;
- cruise subtle;
- acceleration/deceleration contributes more than raw speed;
- airborne chassis body is strongly reduced;
- presentation smoothing is independent of authority/physics state.

### Finite haptics

- boost-start kick: exact accepted vertical-boost authority only;
- gun kick: trusted gun semantic only;
- touchdown: trusted physics touchdown only, gain scaled monotonically from peak downward vertical speed.

REV-8 haptic priority is hard clear > touchdown > gun > boost > continuous chassis.

### Adaptive triggers

- R2 persistent mounted-gun wall under current Tier-A authority;
- one 70 ms mechanical R2 recoil snap per trusted gun semantic;
- R2 returns to the REV-8 wall after recoil expiry;
- L2 persistent slow-time detent while trusted `VehicleAim` is active;
- Data/Pause/Loading, authority loss, vehicle exit, ship takeover, and shutdown neutralize vehicle trigger output;
- no cached aim/recoil is restored after a clear.
