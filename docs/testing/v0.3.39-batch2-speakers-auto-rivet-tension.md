## Hardware acceptance

Launch Starfield normally and allow the background speaker cache to finish. The log should report `familiesReady=35/35`, `profilesReady=36/36`, `variantsPrepared=321/321`, `failedFamilies=0`, and Batch 2 discovery inactive.

Test Old Earth Shotgun, Pacifier, Auto-Rivet, Microgun, Bridger, Negotiator, Magshear, Magpulse, Magsniper, and Magstorm. For each, verify controller-speaker fire plus any reload/draw/holster/mechanical cues that occur during normal use. Normal Starfield audio must remain unchanged.

For Auto-Rivet specifically, slowly pull R2. A light mechanical vibration should begin in the working part of the trigger pull and strengthen as R2 approaches full travel. Hold it briefly, then release/fire: the discharge vibration should be clearly stronger than the held tension. Verify the held vibration stops on release, pausing, switching weapons, and normal game shutdown.

For Microgun, Magshear, and Magstorm, hold automatic fire for several seconds and watch for any recurrence of the pre-v0.3.38 microfreezes. For Magsniper, hold through charge and release several shots.

Exit Starfield normally and retain the newest `StarfieldDualSense(...).log` for acceptance review.
