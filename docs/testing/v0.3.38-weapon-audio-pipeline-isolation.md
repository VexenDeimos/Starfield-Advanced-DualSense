# v0.3.38 Weapon Audio Pipeline Isolation Test

v0.3.38 is a stability rebuild of the weapon-speaker pipeline. It does **not** expand the live arsenal. Its release gate is that controller startup and gameplay remain responsive while weapon-speaker cache preparation and Batch 2 discovery media resolution run on one dedicated background worker.

The live catalog still contains 26 logical weapon profiles and 155 cues. Old Earth Pistol and XM-2311 explicitly share one physical 1911 speaker-audio family, so startup prepares 25 physical families and 230 unique physical variants instead of redundantly requiring 237 logical assignments. Each family is published atomically when complete. A family that is still warming is controller-speaker silent while haptics, adaptive triggers, lightbar, touchpad, controller input, and normal Starfield audio continue normally.

Batch 2 discovery is now gated before capture to this exact set only: Old Earth Shotgun, Pacifier, Auto-Rivet, Microgun, Bridger, Negotiator, Magshear, Magpulse, Magsniper, and Magstorm. Eon, Maelstrom, XM-2311, named variants, and every other non-target weapon must not enter the discovery-resolution queue. The queue is bounded and non-blocking; diagnostic records may be dropped rather than stalling Starfield.

## Hardware acceptance

1. Launch Starfield and load a save. The DualSense/lightbar should become active without waiting for all weapon speaker families to finish warming. A weapon family may be temporarily controller-speaker silent until its own `Weapon audio family ready:` line appears.
2. After their families are ready, test **Eon** and **Maelstrom** fire, reload, draw, and holster. Existing controller-speaker behavior should be unchanged.
3. Rapid-fire Eon and Maelstrom. There should be no obvious weapon-audio discovery microfreezes, and their use should not produce Batch 2 discovery-resolution work.
4. Test **Old Earth Pistol** and **XM-2311**. Both should have controller-speaker audio after the shared 1911 family is ready. Startup should log `profile=XM-2311 family=Old Earth Pistol mode=explicit-shared`.
5. Test one exact Batch 2 target, preferably **Old Earth Shotgun**. Its discovery evidence should resolve in the background and eventually produce the normal resolved event/media diagnostics without a gameplay hitch.
6. Exit Starfield normally. The plugin must stop accepting discovery work, stop producers, cancel queued diagnostics, join the weapon-audio worker, and exit without hanging.

## Expected evidence

A healthy run should include a worker-start line with `families=25 profiles=26 discoveryTargets=10 discoveryQueue=64`, individual `Weapon audio family ready:` lines as families publish, and a final startup summary with `familiesReady=25/25 profilesReady=26/26 variantsPrepared=230/230 failedFamilies=0` when all physical families resolve successfully.

If one family fails, that family should remain speaker-silent while already-published families continue working. A terminal resolver/catalog failure should disable weapon-speaker/discovery work for that session only; it must not take down haptics, adaptive triggers, lightbar, touchpad, controller input, or normal Starfield audio.
