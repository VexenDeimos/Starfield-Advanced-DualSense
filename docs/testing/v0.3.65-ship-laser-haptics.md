## Scope and authority

v0.3.65 promotes only the pulse-laser signature proven by the isolated v0.3.64 W0/R2 hardware run:

```text
event=0xCE7B2EB1
first Wwise post ~= 90-102 ms before first qualifying R2 sample
held native interval ~= 204-214 ms
quick tap = one post
held fire = repeated same-event / same-game-object posts
```

The production gate requires active pilot authority plus exact `0xCE7B2EB1`. The first Wwise-before-R2 post may wait at most 180 ms for real physical R2 proof. Once proven, later held-fire heartbeats must retain the same Wwise game object and held R2. Raw R2 never creates laser fire. Release, DataMenu/PauseMenu, loading invalidation, pilot exit, and shutdown revoke authority.

Each authorized native heartbeat refreshes a 250 ms laser presentation lease. While live, the controller gets:

- continuous laser-energy haptics layered over propulsion/boost at 0.30 base gain;
- one subtle 32 ms `ShipLaserPulseCrest` per real native heartbeat at 0.28 base gain;
- a smooth moderate R2 `ContinuousResistance` firing wall rather than a ballistic snap.

The lease is only a fail-closed liveness bridge across the observed ~208 ms native heartbeat. It does not synthesize a firing cadence.

The v0.3.63-r6 ballistic family stays frozen. Particle, missile, and EM remain inert.

## Focused hardware test

Use the same isolated **pulse laser on W0/R2** that produced the v0.3.64 evidence. Do not fire other weapon groups during this run.

1. Enter the pilot seat and verify normal propulsion and the generic cockpit R2 wall.
2. Tap R2 once. Expect exactly one short laser-energy response: a subtle pulse/crest rather than a ballistic kick.
3. Wait one second. Hold R2 for 2–3 seconds. Expect a smooth continuous energy texture over propulsion with subtle pulse cadence while the laser visibly fires.
4. Release R2. Laser-specific vibration and firing resistance must stop immediately; propulsion may remain.
5. Hold R2 again for 2–3 seconds. The laser presentation must reacquire from fresh native evidence.
6. Open DataMenu while still in the pilot seat. Ship haptic/trigger output must mute immediately. Close it. Stale laser feedback must not resume by itself.
7. Fire one final short burst after closing DataMenu. It must reacquire normally.
8. Optional ballistic regression: fire the previously accepted ballistic W0/R2 setup and confirm its r6 kick/cadence still feels unchanged.
9. Quit normally and send the fresh log plus feel notes.

Accept v0.3.65 only if all are true:

- quick tap gives one clean laser response;
- held fire feels continuous/energetic, not like ballistic recoil;
- the subtle pulse cadence tracks visible/audible native pulses without an artificial extra rhythm;
- release stops laser-specific feedback immediately;
- fresh held fire reacquires;
- DataMenu/PauseMenu mute remains immediate and stale laser state does not return on close;
- propulsion remains layered and is not replaced by laser feedback;
- accepted ballistic r6 behavior remains unchanged;
- no unrelated on-foot/speaker/controller regressions appear.
