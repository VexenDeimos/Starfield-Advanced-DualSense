# v0.3.43 - Sustained Weapon Speaker Audio

## Scope

v0.3.43 promotes Arc Welder and Cutter into the controller-speaker catalog with lifecycle-aware sustained firing audio. Arc Welder uses exact player Wwise `Loop_Play=0xBB87C268` and `Loop_Stop=0x46C5B7DA`; Cutter uses `Loop_Play=0x8EDCB1C7` and `Loop_Stop=0x40FF9B15`. All four events require player game object `0x2` and zero external sources.

The persistent arc/laser body lasts for the full real firing hold with no fixed timeout. Raw R2 is start-no-authority: it cannot fabricate a beam during reload or any other state where Starfield has not posted a fresh Loop_Play. R2 release is stop-only safety. Weapon swap, GamePaused, DataMenu, PauseMenu, LoadingMenu, backend stop, endpoint invalidation, and shutdown also clear the active generation. Closing a menu or reconnecting an endpoint never resumes stale audio.

Expected prepared catalog: **46 logical profiles / 43 physical families / 444 physical variants**. The v0.3.42 finite weapon profiles remain unchanged, discovery stays inactive with zero targets, and normal Starfield audio remains untouched.

## Hardware acceptance

### Arc Welder

1. Equip Arc Welder and confirm its draw sound plays through the controller.
2. Hold R2 for about 2 seconds. Expect one press transient followed by a continuous arc body for the entire hold.
3. Hold R2 for 8-10 seconds. The arc body must not gap, cut off, accumulate overlapping loops, or replay the attack transient.
4. Release R2. The persistent arc must stop immediately and one release transient should play.
5. Reload. Confirm finite reload cues still play. Pressing R2 during reload must **not** fabricate sustained beam audio.
6. Start firing, then pause. The persistent arc must stop immediately. Keep R2 held while unpausing; the arc must **not** resume.
7. Release and press R2 again after unpausing. A fresh real Wwise Loop_Play should authorize a new generation.

### Cutter

1. Equip Cutter and confirm its draw sound plays through the controller.
2. Hold R2 for about 2 seconds. Expect one press transient followed by a continuous laser body for the entire hold.
3. Hold R2 for 8-10 seconds. The laser body must not gap, cut off, accumulate overlapping loops, or replay the attack transient.
4. Release R2. The persistent laser must stop immediately and one release transient should play.
5. If practical, hold through the Cutter's in-game energy depletion. The real Wwise Loop_Stop must end controller audio even if R2 remains physically held.
6. Start firing and weapon-swap while the beam is active. Persistent audio must stop immediately and must not leak into the newly equipped weapon.
7. Exit Starfield normally.

## Runtime log contract

The log must show the v0.3.43 marker, the sustained activation line, `families=43 profiles=46 discoveryTargets=0`, Arc Welder and Cutter family readiness, and final `familiesReady=43/43 profilesReady=46/46 variantsPrepared=444/444 failedFamilies=0`. Discovery must remain inactive with `reason=sustained-speaker-promotion`.

Arc Welder and Cutter should produce bounded sustained start/stop lifecycle lines with their exact Wwise event IDs and monotonically increasing generation numbers. A normal quit must clear persistent state before speaker/backend teardown. No persistent voice may survive pause, weapon swap, endpoint invalidation, backend stop, or shutdown.
