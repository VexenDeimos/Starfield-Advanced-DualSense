# v0.3.42 - Batch 3 Energy Speakers Phase A

## Scope

This build promotes the v0.3.41 discovery evidence for Solstice, Orion, Novalight, Va'ruun Starshard, Va'ruun Inflictor, Novablast Disruptor, Va'ruun Quickstrike, and Va'ruun Longfang. Quickstrike shares Solstice's physical audio family and Longfang shares Orion's. Arc Welder and Cutter remain outside the speaker catalog for the next sustained start/loop/stop lifecycle phase.

Expected catalog: **44 logical profiles / 273 cues / 41 physical families / 416 physical variants**. Batch 3 discovery should be inactive.

## Hardware pass

Test Solstice, Orion, Novalight, Va'ruun Starshard, Va'ruun Inflictor, Novablast Disruptor, Va'ruun Quickstrike, and Va'ruun Longfang. For each weapon, verify draw, fire, reload, and holster controller-speaker audio. Quickstrike should retain its own rapid weapon behavior while using the Solstice speaker family; Longfang should retain its own burst behavior while using the Orion speaker family.

For Novablast, verify that a completed discharge produces the discrete controller-speaker shot and that merely charging the weapon does not repeatedly trigger the one-shot fire cue.

Arc Welder and Cutter are expected to remain without controller-speaker firing audio in this build. Their existing haptics should continue to work.

After the pass, exit Starfield normally and inspect the log for `familiesReady=41/41`, `profilesReady=44/44`, `variantsPrepared=416/416`, `failedFamilies=0`, and `Weapon SFX discovery: INACTIVE reason=batch3-phase-a-promotion`.
