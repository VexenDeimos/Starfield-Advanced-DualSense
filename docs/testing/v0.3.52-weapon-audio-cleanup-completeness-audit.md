# v0.3.52 Weapon Audio Cleanup + Completeness Audit

v0.3.52 is a cleanup/hardening release after the Va'ruun Starstorm sustained-body tuning was accepted at gain `1.60`.

The temporary v0.3.50 Starstorm runtime diagnostics are removed from the shipping path: no prepared-PCM window logging, no persistent-render checkpoint logging, and no diagnostic media-id metadata is carried through persistent speaker voices. The historical PCM diagnostic helper and focused v0.3.50 unit test remain available only as a test artifact; they are no longer linked into the plugin, haptics tests, transport tests, or the weapon-audio pipeline tests.

No accepted audio behavior changes in this release. Starstorm sustained-body media `963157375` remains at `1.60`; its start/stop/reload/equip cues remain unchanged. Penumbra, Starlash, Arc Welder, Cutter, all other weapon speaker profiles, haptics, adaptive triggers, VO, and normal Starfield audio are unchanged.

A new completeness audit drives the entire catalog through the real `WeaponAudioPipeline` preparation/publication path with a deterministic fake decoder backend and verifies:

- 49 logical weapon speaker profiles;
- 45 physical audio families;
- 495 physical prepared variants;
- exactly four intentional logical-family shares:
  - `XM-2311 -> Old Earth Pistol`
  - `Va'ruun Starlash -> Equinox`
  - `Va'ruun Quickstrike -> Solstice`
  - `Va'ruun Longfang -> Orion`
- every logical profile resolves to a canonical physical family;
- all 45 physical families publish;
- zero families fail preparation/publication;
- all 49 logical profiles resolve to ready prepared families;
- all 495 physical variants reach the prepared cache.
