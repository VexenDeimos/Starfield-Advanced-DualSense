# v0.3.41 Batch 3 Energy-Weapon Discovery

## Purpose

Collect one clean hardware run for the ten exact Batch 3 energy weapons without changing the accepted v0.3.40 haptics or the 36-profile controller-speaker catalog. Discovery is diagnostic-only. The ten targets are:

1. Solstice
2. Orion
3. Novalight
4. Va'ruun Starshard
5. Va'ruun Inflictor
6. Arc Welder
7. Cutter
8. Novablast Disruptor
9. Va'ruun Quickstrike
10. Va'ruun Longfang

## Hardware capture

Use one Starfield session. Equip the targets in the order listed above. For each weapon, exercise draw, fire, reload where applicable, and holster. Give the discovery windows roughly one second between major actions.

For sustained/charge weapons:

- Arc Welder: hold a sustained discharge for about one second, then release.
- Cutter: hold the beam for about one second, then release.
- Novablast Disruptor: perform at least two full charge/release shots.
- Va'ruun Quickstrike and Va'ruun Longfang: include a short rapid burst if the equipped configuration supports rapid fire.

Existing haptics should continue to behave normally. New controller-speaker weapon audio is **not** expected for these ten targets yet; this run is collecting the Wwise evidence needed for the following profile-promotion build.

After all ten are exercised, quit Starfield normally and preserve the resulting `StarfieldDualSense(...).log`. Acceptance requires discovery evidence for all ten exact targets and no discovery work for unrelated/promoted weapons.
