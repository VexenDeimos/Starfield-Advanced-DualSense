# v0.3.71-r2 Ship EM Trigger Rearm

## Scope

This is a bounded EM trigger-delivery fix only. It does not change EM firing authority, Wwise identity, haptic waveform/gain, native cadence, or any accepted ballistic/laser/particle/missile behavior.

Runtime marker: `0.3.71-ship-em-haptics-r2-trigger-rearm`  
ProductVersion/FileVersion: `0.3.71.0`

## Exact r2 behavior

- Accepted r1 EM body pulse stays 75 ms at 0.70 gain.
- Accepted r1 R2 electrical break stays start 72, forces 132/196/112, frequency 92, lifetime 90 ms.
- EM trigger partial-rearm threshold is 48. The normal R2 full-release threshold remains 12.
- After each confirmed EM shot, the EM trigger presentation is considered un-rearmed until R2 reaches 48 or below.
- A new authoritative EM shot while un-rearmed first outputs one neutral trigger frame. On the following controller cycle, the exact r1 EM `EffectEx` is applied for 90 ms.
- No synthetic firing timer or cadence is added; only an already-authorized native EM shot may start the refresh/retrigger sequence.
- Menu, loading, pilot invalidation, shutdown, and competing accepted ship-family effects clear any pending EM refresh.

## Hardware test

Use the same isolated EM/Suppressor weapon on W0/R2.

1. Fire 5 normal single shots with a comfortable full release between shots.
2. Fire 10-15 rapid taps while intentionally **not** allowing R2 to return fully to rest between taps. Try to reproduce the old failure by bouncing the trigger through a shallow partial-release motion.
3. Do 5 more taps where you deliberately back R2 out roughly one quarter of its travel before pressing again; a complete release should not be necessary.
4. Hold R2 for several native EM heartbeats and confirm the accepted held-fire feel remains unchanged.
5. Open and close DataMenu, then fire again.

Success criteria:

- Every authoritative EM discharge still produces the accepted 75 ms body `bzz-ZAP`.
- Rapid partial-release taps no longer leave the R2 electrical break feeling dead until a full release.
- No extra trigger pulse occurs without a real native EM discharge.
- Held-fire cadence remains native and unchanged.
- DataMenu hard-mutes and fresh post-menu fire reacquires normally.
