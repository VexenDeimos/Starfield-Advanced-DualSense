# v0.3.78 Ship Launch & Landing Rumble — Hardware Gate

## Scope

v0.3.78 adds one shared continuous ship rumble around the already-verified launch/landing lifecycle. It deliberately reuses the accepted `ShipPropulsion` continuous texture at gain `0.30` and level `0.55`; the propulsion mapping itself is unchanged.

- Takeoff start: current pilot context + fresh landed/non-docked state + `FaderMenu` open while `TakeoffMenu` is closed.
- Takeoff stop: verified `landed=true -> landed=false` boundary, or cancellation/interruption.
- Landing start: first landing `FaderMenu` has closed and the existing precision touchdown tail is armed.
- Landing stop: verified `landed=false -> landed=true` boundary, or cancellation/interruption.
- Touchdown: accepted v0.3.77 `ShipTouchdownThump`, 100 ms at 0.90 gain, unchanged.
- No adaptive-trigger, speaker, or lightbar changes.

## One-pass hardware check

1. Launch Starfield with the wired DualSense and confirm the startup marker is `0.3.78-ship-launch-landing-rumble`.
2. Enter the pilot seat on a landed, non-docked ship and take off normally.
3. Confirm a continuous low/heavy rumble is present during the takeoff transition and stops when the ship becomes airborne. It must not remain stuck in normal flight.
4. Fly to a location that gives a full visible landing cinematic and land normally.
5. Confirm a continuous low/heavy rumble is present during the visible descent.
6. At touchdown, confirm the continuous rumble stops and the existing single heavy touchdown thump still fires once.
7. Remain seated for about five seconds after landing. Confirm no continuous transition rumble remains stuck.
8. Quit normally and send the resulting `StarfieldDualSense(...).log` plus whether the takeoff rumble, landing rumble, and touchdown thump were each felt.

## Expected log markers

The successful path should contain lines equivalent to:

```text
Ship launch/landing rumble: stage=submitted action=start phase=takeoff kind=ShipPropulsion gain=0.300 level=0.550
Ship launch/landing rumble: stage=submitted action=stop phase=takeoff-boundary kind=None gain=0.000 level=0.000
Ship launch/landing rumble: stage=submitted action=start phase=landing kind=ShipPropulsion gain=0.300 level=0.550
Ship launch/landing rumble: stage=submitted action=stop phase=touchdown-boundary kind=None gain=0.000 level=0.000
Ship touchdown haptics: stage=submitted kind=ShipTouchdownThump gain=0.900 ...
```

If a transition is cancelled, the stop reason may instead be `takeoff-cancelled` or `landing-cancelled`. A cancelled sequence must leave the continuous haptic state neutral.
