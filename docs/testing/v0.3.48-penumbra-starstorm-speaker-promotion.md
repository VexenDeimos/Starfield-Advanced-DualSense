## Hardware acceptance

### Va'ruun Penumbra

1. Fire 8-10 normal individual shots with a short pause between shots. Each launch should sound complete, with natural variation but no thin/partial rotating components.
2. Reload twice and verify the bolt-open, bullet-handling, mag-in sequence sounds natural through the controller speaker.
3. Holster/draw twice.
4. Hold R2 as you normally would for the Penumbra's existing charge/stress behavior. No new controller-speaker charge loop is expected in v0.3.48; only the actual launch is promoted.

### Va'ruun Starstorm

1. Fire 4-6 short bursts of about 1-2 seconds, fully releasing R2 between bursts.
2. Fire two long bursts of 8-10 seconds. The sustained body must remain continuous and stable after the initial attack and must not restart/rotate while R2 stays held.
3. Do several very short 0.2-0.5 second taps. Listen specifically for any start accent that lingers unnaturally after release.
4. Release R2 after a long burst. The sustained body must stop promptly and the stop/power-down components should sound natural.
5. While firing, swap weapons once and open a blocking menu once. Persistent audio must not remain stuck.
6. Reload twice and holster/draw twice.

### Baseline sanity

Quickly fire Starlash once or twice and, if convenient, one Arc Welder/Cutter hold. Their previously accepted behavior must remain unchanged.

Quit Starfield normally and send the fresh `StarfieldDualSense` log.
