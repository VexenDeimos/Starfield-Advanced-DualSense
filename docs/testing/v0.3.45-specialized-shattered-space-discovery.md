# v0.3.45 - Specialized Shattered Space Weapon Discovery

## Scope

v0.3.45 is a diagnostic-only exact-target discovery pass for three specialized Shattered Space weapons:

1. Va'ruun Starlash
2. Va'ruun Penumbra
3. Va'ruun Starstorm

No controller-speaker profile is added for these weapons in this build. The accepted v0.3.44 Arc Welder/Cutter layered sustained speaker implementation remains unchanged. Existing haptics, adaptive triggers, the 46-profile / 43-family / 444-variant speaker catalog, and normal Starfield audio remain unchanged.

Discovery is armed only while one of the three exact target identities is equipped. Event/media resolution stays on the existing background weapon-audio worker and the existing bounded discovery queue. The purpose is to determine the real Wwise shape before deciding whether each weapon belongs in a one-shot, charge/release, sustained, layered, or hybrid speaker path.

## One-session hardware capture

Wait for the weapon audio pipeline startup-complete line before beginning the three target tests. Use one Starfield session and leave roughly one second between major draw/fire/reload/holster actions so the discovery windows are easy to curate.

### Va'ruun Starlash

- Equip/draw it.
- Fire at least six separate precision shots at a moderate pace.
- Perform a reload after firing enough rounds to make it meaningful.
- Fire another three shots.
- Holster or swap away.

### Va'ruun Penumbra

- Equip/draw it.
- Perform at least three short trigger pulls/releases.
- Perform at least three deliberately longer held-trigger launches before release.
- Reload if the weapon supports/needs it in that state.
- Holster or swap away.

The goal is to capture whether the audio includes a distinct charge/start/loop/stop lifecycle in addition to the actual launch/discharge event. Existing Penumbra haptics should continue to behave normally.

### Va'ruun Starstorm

- Equip/draw it.
- Fire at least three short bursts.
- Fire at least two longer sustained bursts of roughly 3-5 seconds.
- Reload.
- Fire one additional long burst.
- Holster or swap away.

The goal is to determine whether Starstorm uses rotating fire variants, simultaneous layers, a true start/loop/stop lifecycle, or a hybrid.

After all three weapons are exercised, quit Starfield normally and preserve the resulting `StarfieldDualSense(...).log`.

## Runtime log contract

Startup must show `StarfieldDualSense 0.3.45-specialized-shattered-space-discovery`, discovery `ACTIVE` with `batch=4-specialized-shattered-space operatorTargets=3`, and the weapon-audio worker with `discoveryTargets=3`. The existing speaker cache must still complete at **43/43 families, 46/46 profiles, 444/444 variants, failedFamilies=0**.

Acceptance requires discovery evidence for all three exact target weapons and no exact-target discovery work for unrelated/promoted weapons. The build is not a speaker-audio acceptance build; it is evidence collection for the following promotion release.
