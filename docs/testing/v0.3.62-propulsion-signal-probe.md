## Startup proof

Near runtime startup, look for either:

`Ship flight-control probe: observation hook installed ... lanes=throttleTarget+0x68,effectiveThrottle+0x6C,velocity+0x70 behavior=passive`

or a fail-soft message explaining that passive writer capture was unavailable. A missing or ambiguous signature must never be guessed.

## Short flight capture

1. Start on foot with the Maelstrom (or another known weapon) and confirm its accepted trigger/haptics.
2. Enter the pilot seat and take off. Once fully in space, leave thrust near neutral / coast for about **5 seconds**.
3. Apply normal forward thrust from low speed for about **5 seconds**.
4. Hold steady cruise for about **5 seconds**.
5. Release thrust and coast for about **5 seconds**.
6. Apply braking/deceleration for about **5 seconds**.
7. Trigger one clear boost for about **3-5 seconds**, then release it.
8. Leave the pilot seat and verify the normal on-foot trigger/lightbar return from fresh context.
9. Quit normally and provide the full `StarfieldDualSense(...).log`.

## What the log should contain

While pilot authority is active, the useful lines begin with:

`Ship propulsion probe r2:`

A successful native capture should report a nonzero `cluster` and readable:

- `throttleTarget` — candidate native throttle target at `+0x68`
- `effectiveThrottle` — candidate native effective throttle at `+0x6C`
- `velocity` — candidate ship velocity at `+0x70`
- `boostFuelCurrent` / `boostFuelPermanent` — already-proven boost authority

The test succeeds as reconnaissance if at least one native throttle/velocity lane correlates cleanly with neutral, acceleration, cruise/coast, and braking. Boost fuel should again fall only during boost and rise during recharge.

## Acceptance rule

Do not tune propulsion haptics until the hardware log proves a reliable game-side normal-propulsion signal. If the native writer signature is unavailable or the candidate lanes do not correlate cleanly, stop and refine acquisition instead of substituting raw controller input.
