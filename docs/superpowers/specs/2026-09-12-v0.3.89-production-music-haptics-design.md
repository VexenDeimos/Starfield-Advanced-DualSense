# v0.3.89 Production Music Haptics Design

## Status
Approved in chat after v0.3.88 proved selected-media authority on September 12, 2026.

## Goal
Turn Starfield's authoritative selected score media into bounded DualSense music haptics without whole-game audio capture and without weakening existing gameplay haptic authority.

## Proven authority
v0.3.88 proved that catalog-qualified `Starfield_MUS` events already carry Starfield-owned `AK_EndOfEvent | AK_Duration` callbacks. The existing cookie-preserving callback chain exposes the exact selected `mediaId` while forwarding Starfield's callback unchanged. Production music haptics will use only those selected-media callbacks as score authority.

## Runtime architecture
1. Keep the v0.3.88 catalog-driven target set and cookie-preserving callback chain.
2. Convert each selected-media callback into a tiny deferred `MusicSelectionObservation`; the Wwise callback thread performs no resolver, decode, filesystem, allocation-heavy haptic work, or controller output.
3. Send the exact `(eventId, mediaId, playingId, selectedAt)` to the existing audio pipeline worker at higher priority than diagnostic music recon/discovery work.
4. Resolve and decode only the selected WEM. Do not decode all media for all 124 catalog events. Use weak decoded-PCM reuse so concurrent/repeated selections can share live PCM without permanently retaining an unbounded score cache.
5. Return a prepared stereo 48 kHz PCM voice to the runtime. Compute an age-derived start frame so decode latency skips already-elapsed audio instead of restarting the haptic segment late from frame zero.
6. Submit the voice to the shared DualSense audio transport. The transport owns a dedicated bounded `MusicHapticsMixer` independent of the existing gameplay `HapticMixer`.
7. Render gameplay haptics first. Render music to a separate block, then side-chain duck the music block from the already-rendered gameplay magnitude before adding it. Existing gameplay output therefore has priority.

## Music tactile mapping
The first production version intentionally avoids FFTs or complex psychoacoustic analysis.

- Each active score voice reads the exact decoded stereo PCM selected by Wwise.
- Per-channel absolute sample energy feeds a simple envelope follower (fast attack, slower release) so the controller follows musical swells, accents, and rhythm rather than reproducing high-frequency score audio directly as actuator noise.
- Stereo-ish mapping uses 85/15 crossfeed between left and right envelopes.
- The envelope drives a low-frequency tactile carrier. Both actuators use the same carrier phase/frequency; left/right intensity differences preserve stereo placement without creating distracting inter-actuator beating.
- Each voice fades in over 350 ms measured from its authoritative selected-media start. If decoding completes late, the fade begins at the age-equivalent point rather than restarting from zero.
- The combined music layer is hard-capped at 0.65 peak before gameplay-priority ducking and is also scaled by global `HapticStrength`.
- Maximum simultaneously active music voices: 32. Overflow fails soft by dropping the newest voice and incrementing a diagnostic counter.

## Gameplay priority
Music must never become the dominant effect during authored gameplay haptics.

For each render frame, measure the existing gameplay actuator magnitude before music is added. As gameplay magnitude rises, linearly reduce the music contribution; at sufficiently strong gameplay output the music layer reaches zero. This keeps weapon recoil, damage, ship, REV-8, launch/landing, and other existing haptics authoritative without modifying their waveforms or gain values.

## Menus and lifecycle
`MusicHapticsEnabled` is a separate config toggle and defaults to true. Production music haptics require both `AdvancedHaptics=true` and `MusicHapticsEnabled=true`.

Hard-blocking menu/lifecycle boundaries for v0.3.89:
- MainMenu
- DataMenu
- PauseMenu
- LoadingMenu
- FaderMenu
- Shutdown

Opening a blocking menu clears every active/pending music voice immediately. Closing a menu does not restore stale voices. Only a fresh selected-media callback can reacquire music haptics.

An `AK_EndOfEvent` observation clears active voices for that Wwise `playingId`. Natural PCM exhaustion also retires voices.

## Failure behavior
- Missing target catalog: no selection chaining, no music haptics.
- Callback chain context unavailable/ambiguous: Starfield callback remains untouched; no music haptic authority is fabricated.
- Selected media resolver/decode failure: drop that music voice only.
- Worker/result queue full: drop newest production music request/result; never block Wwise or gameplay threads.
- Music transport queue full: drop newest music voice; gameplay haptics continue.
- Endpoint invalidation or haptics client stop: clear music voices with the rest of haptic transport state.
- `MusicHapticsEnabled=false`: no production music requests or output; diagnostic recon may still operate when DebugLogging is enabled.

## Logging
Production logging is compact. With DebugLogging enabled, log selected-media preparation/submission/drop reasons and aggregate queue drops. Do not retain the giant full-event media diagnostic dump as a prerequisite for production output.

## Non-goals for v0.3.89
- No whole-game loopback/mix capture.
- No score FFT or beat detector.
- No user-exposed music strength slider beyond global `HapticStrength` and the fixed 0.65 cap.
- No predecode of all music media.
- No changes to adaptive triggers, lightbar, controller speaker, or existing gameplay haptic waveforms.
